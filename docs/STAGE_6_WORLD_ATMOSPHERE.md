# Stage 6 — World and Atmosphere

Initial forest atmosphere pass implemented:
- Deterministic scatter of low-poly rocks, shrubs and deadwood silhouettes to reduce the empty procedural-floor look.
- Tuned night fog and moonlight, including a bounded directional shadow distance.
- Existing mobile graphics quality profiles continue to control fog and shadows.

Still outstanding for this stage:
- Collision-aware forest paths and navigation validation.
- Ambient wind, insects, distant wildlife and spatial audio assets.
- Additional lighting and performance tuning on real Android hardware.
- Runtime QA; successful export workflows do not establish gameplay correctness.
