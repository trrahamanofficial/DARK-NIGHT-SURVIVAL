# Stage 10 — Game Modes and Difficulty

## Implemented first pass
- Persistent offline mode/difficulty selection and pause-menu selector.
- Story Campaign: existing story objective flow runs only in this mode.
- Survival Mode: timed escalating enemy waves.
- Free Explore: no enemy spawning or story objective triggers.
- Nightmare Mode: extra Stalker threat and increased enemy health, damage and detection.
- Custom Mode: selected difficulty with balanced default rules; individual custom sliders are not yet implemented.
- Easy, Normal, Hard and Nightmare profiles affect enemy damage, health, detection and resource pickup amounts.
- Dynamic difficulty adjusts enemy damage according to player health.
- Co-op is shown as Coming Soon and cannot be selected as an active mode; multiplayer is not claimed.

## Remaining
- Runtime balancing and mode-specific win/loss conditions.
- Full Custom Mode rule editor.
- Survival wave pacing playtests and HUD verification.
- Dedicated map-specific objectives per mode.
- Android/Web export verification and physical runtime QA.
